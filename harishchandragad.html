Plan Your Journey

Day Wise Itinerary

Day 1

Pune → Harishchandragad Base → Trek

  • Early morning pickup from Pune or your selected pickup location.
  • Travel towards the Harishchandragad base village.
  • Breakfast stop during the journey.
  • Start the trek through the Sahyadri landscape.
  • Explore the fort surroundings and ancient structures.
  • Visit Kedareshwar Cave and the ancient Shiva temple.
  • Explore nearby fort areas and scenic viewpoints.
  • Evening rest and overnight stay/camping as per the selected arrangement.
Day 2

Konkan Kada → Harishchandragad → Return to Pune

  • Early morning start for scenic viewpoints.
  • Visit the famous Konkan Kada.
  • Enjoy panoramic views of the Sahyadri ranges.
  • Photography and exploration around the fort.
  • Breakfast and preparation for the descent.
  • Trek down towards the base village.
  • Lunch break after completing the trek.
  • Begin the return journey towards Pune.
  • Drop at your selected location in Pune.
